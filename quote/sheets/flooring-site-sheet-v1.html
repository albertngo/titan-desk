How each box becomes the quote

R1–R12One room each on the Rooms tab. Measured with comes from R0, once for the sheet.
New floorEach floor gets its own Install and Material lines at the sum of its rooms' sq ft, labelled with those rooms or their level ("Upper: vinyl", "Main: engineered"). A blank room gets every floor written below.
Blank or bothWith two floors written, those rooms are a choice, so the quote gets two options: one with Floor 1 there, one with Floor 2. A room ticked 1 or 2 stays that way in both.
RemoveTicked rooms, grouped by Floor there now: Carpet or Lam/Vin → "Carpet / Laminate / Vinyl"; Wood → "Hardwood / Parquet"; Tile/ply → "Tiles on Plywood"; Tile/conc → "Tiles on Concrete". No tick, no removal line.
W1One tick names the material and its install. Vinyl, laminate and engineered click → the click install; tile → tiles; herringbone vinyl → herringbone; engineered ¾", solid and herringbone engineered take the Nail down / Nail + glue tick. Sand & stain and Buff & polish have no material.
W2Standard (or blank) uses the quick form's standard product for that floor (e.g. STD-LVP-7MM for vinyl click). Customer supplies: install only, no material line. SKU: that catalogue product, named in the car check; a SKU not in the catalogue comes in unpriced, never guessed.
W3Baseboard lines at the sum of Linear ft for the rooms. Reinstall uses the Labor rows, New uses Mat + Labor. No Linear ft written: the quick form's ⅓-of-sq-ft default, flagged.
W4–W5Subfloor and Disposal lines. Disposal left blank means no disposal line (the customer takes it away). Anything with no Rate Card row comes in unpriced with a note.

Three ways to tick it

One floorFill Floor 1. No ticks in New floor. One option.
Vinyl or hardwood everywhereFloor 1 vinyl, Floor 2 hardwood. No ticks. Two options to compare. The usual case.
Vinyl up, engineered mainFloor 1 vinyl, Floor 2 engineered. Upper rooms tick 1, main rooms tick 2. One option, one price, both floors in it.

In the car

  1. Open the quote, press Prepare measured quote. The app finds the Site Sheet photos in CompanyCam.
  2. Every field is shown on this sheet's picture beside its photo (your call: all fields, at first). Boxes Claude wasn't sure of are washed yellow.
  3. The new total sits beside the preliminary's, with any warnings.
  4. Send measured. The follow-up tags and marketing links go out as they do today.

Settled 2026-11-03

  • Two floors on one sheet are enough.
  • Floors are set room by room, as the PM measures. A blank room gets every floor written, so "vinyl or hardwood everywhere" needs no ticks; different levels tick 1 or 2.
  • Blank Remove or Disposal means the customer does it: no line.
  • Transitions, furniture and appliances are off for now; added later if needed.
  • Tile on plywood and tile on concrete are recorded apart. Open: both choices point at the same Rate Card row today
  • Material: standard by default, customer supplies, or a SKU written on site.
  • Kept simple so the team can start with it.
  • Herringbone engineered has its own Install row in Quick Quote Choices (added 2026-11-03). Open: link its Rate Card row, then tick Active
