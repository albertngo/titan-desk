Follow up: staff guide

Titan Quotes · Your morning brief · v3, November 2026

What changed since v2

  • Follow up is now a morning brief. One line per person, with one button that does the next step. Everything else is behind the arrow.
  • Three groups: Reach today, If you have time, and Later (folded).
  • What you've done stays on the list, ticked and greyed, until tomorrow. The top line counts how many are done.
  • You can change the next step, and leave a note for the brief on a customer.
  • When you do something other than the suggestion, the app may ask why in one tap. It's optional, and it's how the suggestions get better.
  • The conversation panel opens when you open a person, not before.

Reading your morning

The top line says your day in one sentence, for example "Joey, 6 people today: 2 texts, 1 call, 1 visit to book. 2 done." The name buttons beside it (Pourya | Joey | Albert | Everyone) switch whose day it is.

Done for today

A row ticks off when anything reaches the customer today: a Send, a text, email or call in GHL, I followed up, Snooze, Postpone or Lost. It greys, says what was done (✓ Texted 9:14 AM) and moves under the ones still to do. Quotes marked Lost stay, ticked, until tomorrow.

When you disagree with the brief

  • Change the step: open the person and pick another in Next step. The brief is rewritten for it, draft included.
  • Note for the brief: open the person and add a note, like "wife decides, call evenings". Every brief for this customer follows it, on this quote and their next. It also goes into their GHL conversation as an internal comment.
  • Why? When you snooze a suggested text, or pick a call instead, the app may ask why: Not ready, Prefer to call, Wrong read, Already handled. Tap one or skip it.
How the app learns. Every button records what was suggested and what you did. The next brief for that customer is told your note and the last three times you went against it, so it doesn't repeat a step you turned down. Albert reviews the patterns; the rules only change when he approves a change.

The score, the flags and the reasons a quote is due are the same as in v2.

Rules

  1. Owed first, then the rest of Reach today, then If you have time.
  2. Open the person and read the conversation before you reach out.
  3. Check the preview before Send now. It goes to a real customer and can't be taken back.
  4. Log what GHL can't see with I followed up.
  5. Lost only on the customer's word. Postpone only to a date they gave; otherwise Snooze.
  6. Tell the brief what it should know with a note. Tap a reason when you disagree.
  7. Never share internal notes or costs with a customer.

A good five minutes

  1. Open Follow up. Read the top line.
  2. Work Reach today top to bottom: press the button, or open the person if you need more.
  3. Then If you have time.
  4. Done when every line in Reach today has a tick.
