Follow up: staff guide

Titan Quotes · How to work the Follow up page · v2, November 2026

What changed since v1

  • Every quote has a score from 0 to 100 and a tier: Needs you Watch Auto.
  • Preliminaries mostly run on their own. GHL's texts handle them until the customer shows real interest.
  • Measured quotes are sent from the app. Edit the draft, press Send, check the preview, Send now.
  • The red number on the top bar counts only what needs you today.
  • Snoozed has its own tab. Briefs refresh every day on their own.

What the page is for

Follow up (top bar) lists every quote we've sent that is still waiting on the customer, sorted by who needs a person most. A quote leaves the list when the customer accepts it, or when you mark it Lost or Postpone it.

The score

Each row shows a number from 0 to 100 and a bar . Read it as roughly the chance this quote closes. The two marks on the bar show where Watch (8) and Needs you (20) start, so you can see how close a quote is to moving up. The reasons are written beside it, for example "14 two-way messages, 6 from them · visited · $18k".

What moves itWhy
Back-and-forthThe more real conversation, the higher. Our numbers: quotes with 5 or fewer two-way messages close about 3% of the time; 11 to 20 messages, about 20%; 21 to 40, about 33%. Automated texts don't count. Messages from the customer count more than ours.
VisitsA booked visit lifts it. A visit that happened lifts it to at least 28. Home and store both lifts it to at least 60: those close 4 in 5.
OpensOpening the quote recently, or on several days, lifts it.
QuietA week with no word from them lowers it; two weeks lowers it more. Not if they told us when to come back.
ValueBigger jobs are worth more of our time: under $3k counts a little less, over $10k and $25k a little more.

The tiers

TierWhat it means
Needs youA score of 20 or more, or one of the flags below. Work these first. Up to 8 a day each; any more wait under Next up.
WatchSome interest, not hot yet. Follow up when they're due.
On autoPreliminaries with little conversation. GHL's texts are handling them (day 3 "any questions?", day 7 "book a visit"). Folded at the bottom. They move up on their own the moment the customer engages. You don't need to touch them.

Flags: these always go to Needs you, whatever the score:

  • They asked about price, dates or availability, or named a day, and we haven't answered.
  • They mentioned another company or quote, or pushed back ("too high", "need to think").
  • A $25k+ or commercial job.
  • They went quiet after a long conversation.
  • They're unhappy with us.
  • The quote didn't arrive: GHL says the text or email failed. Check the number or email and resend.
  • A measured quote nobody has opened 4 days after sending. Call them.
  • They keep reopening the quote without replying. They're deciding: reach out today.

Measured quotes never go on auto: a person always has them. The score only sets their order.

The brief

Each quote has a short brief written from the quote and the GHL conversation: where it stands, what they asked (✓ answered, ? not yet), the next step and a draft message. It is rewritten every day and whenever something new happens; Rewrite asks for a fresh one now.

  • Open slots: when a visit is the next step, the draft can offer a real open time from the measure calendar ("We have an opening Thursday at 2pm. Want me to hold it?"). It never says we're in the area or names their city unless they told us where they are.
  • Home and store: a customer measured at home but not yet in the store gets a showroom invite. Seen in the store but not measured gets offered the measure.
The brief is a suggestion, not a script. Read the conversation first. Never send a promise of a price, date or discount nobody agreed to.

Sending a follow-up (measured quotes)

  1. Click the row. Read the conversation and the brief.
  2. Edit the draft in the box if anything is off.
  3. Press Send text or Send email. The app picks the channel the customer last used, otherwise a text.
  4. Check the preview: who it goes to, that it's from your own number or address, and the exact words. Press Send now, or Cancel.

It goes into their GHL conversation right away, counts as a follow-up, and is noted in the quote's History. The app refuses a message with a price that isn't on the quote. Preliminaries still use Copy and open GHL.

Auto follow-ups box: on each measured quote. The app sends nothing on its own yet. When Albert switches automatic sending on, routine check-ins go out by themselves, never when the customer is waiting on us, never with a flag up, never on $25k+ jobs, only 9am to 7pm Monday to Saturday. Untick the box to keep a quote fully manual.

Rules

  1. Replies first, then Needs you, then visits.
  2. Work your own quotes. If you cover someone else's, say so in your note.
  3. Read the conversation before every follow-up.
  4. Check the preview before Send now. It goes to a real customer and can't be taken back.
  5. Log what GHL can't see with I followed up.
  6. Lost only on the customer's word. Pick the honest reason.
  7. Postpone only to a date they gave. Otherwise Snooze.
  8. Big jobs get a call, not just a text.
  9. Leave "On auto" alone. GHL is handling them; they come to you when they're ready.
  10. Never share internal notes or costs with a customer.

A good five minutes

  1. Open Follow up, filter on your name.
  2. Work Needs you top to bottom: click, read, then call or Send.
  3. Then Watch, if you have time.
  4. Record the outcome: nothing if it was in GHL or sent from the app, otherwise I followed up, Snooze, Postpone or Lost.
